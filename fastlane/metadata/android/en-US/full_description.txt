VeVak lets people you choose request your location on demand by SMS.

You stay in control:
• up to five contacts configured locally;
• a passphrase and time-limited authorisation for each contact;
• immediate revocation from your phone;
• device-wide anti-tracking limits of 15 minutes between replies and 4 replies per 24 hours;
• a discreet offer of additional protection after two valid SMS messages from the same contact.

VeVak needs no VeVak account or mandatory server for its core SMS flow. It contains no advertising, trackers or telemetry.

Location is accessed only when needed for an authorised request or an action you start. VeVak can retain one latest location, replacing the previous one, but does not build a route or location history.

Optional features include a coarse network/IP fallback, one-slot periodic location refresh, local Home network recognition, manual sharing, a discreet emergency shortcut with explicitly selected recipients, and encrypted settings backup.

VeVak does not contact emergency services and does not replace 112, 911 or Android’s built-in emergency features. SMS sending and delivery depend on the device and mobile carrier.
